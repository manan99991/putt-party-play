Tenzy Privacy Policy

Short version: what you do in Tenzy stays on your phone. There is no account and no server.

Stays on your phone

  • Photos, videos, PDFs and files you open in a helper, and the results — until you save or share them yourself
  • Scans, extracted text, receipts, warranties and the notes in your Vault
  • Voice recordings and transcripts you make in Voice to Notes or Rest, only when you tap record
  • Screenshots Memory indexes (the index is built and kept on your phone)
  • Your settings, theme and sticky notes
  • Medicines, reminders, saved letters, measurements, watched web pages, contact marks (favorite, emergency, important) and the helpers you build
Stored in the app's private storage. Deleting the app deletes all of it. We can't see it.

Never collected

  • Your name, email, or any account — there isn't one
  • Analytics, tracking of what you do inside the app, or crash reports
  • Ads, or an advertising identifier — Tenzy has no ads and no ad code
  • Your contacts, location, photos or recordings — they are used on your phone to do the job you asked for and are never sent to us

Permissions, and why

  • Camera: Scan, Studio, Measure, Pro Camera, Identify, Translate, See & Read, Color Vision
  • Microphone: Audio Lab, Voice to Notes, Translate voice mode, Rest, Meeting Notes and Pro Camera video. Levels are measured on your phone; recordings are saved only when you start them
  • Speech recognition: transcribes your voice using Apple's speech recognition. On supported iPhones this runs on the device; otherwise iOS may send the audio to Apple under Apple's privacy policy
  • Photos: helpers you pick photos in (Studio, Privacy Scrubber, Cleaner, Memory). iOS asks you to confirm every deletion
  • Media & Apple Music (iPhone): only when you tap "Show songs from the Music app" in Animate, to put one of your songs under a video you make. Tenzy lists songs stored on your phone that aren't copy-protected, reads them on your phone, and never uploads them. Streamed Apple Music songs are never shown or copied
  • Contacts: the Contacts helper searches, groups, edits, exports and backs up your contacts, finds and merges duplicates, and reminds you of birthdays — only when you use it, on your phone. Edits are saved to your own contacts and the accounts they already sync with (iCloud, Gmail, Outlook)
  • Location (while in use): compass true north, altitude, the GPS Dashboard and parked-car distance, the weather in Daily Brief, travel times and arrival reminders in Untangle and Contacts, and the Location step in Build your own. It stays on your phone; arrival reminders are watched by iOS itself. If you turn on "Save my spot automatically" in the GPS Dashboard, Tenzy keeps using this same While Using location in the background (iPhone shows the blue location indicator) to notice when you stop driving and save where you parked. Tenzy never asks for Always location. It stays on your phone and is never uploaded; turn it off any time in the same place
  • Motion: level, barometer and metal-detector readings in Measure, and, only if you turn on "Save my spot automatically", telling when a drive ends. It stays on your phone
  • Face ID: unlocks the Vault. Tenzy never sees your face data
  • Notifications: only the reminders you turn on (medicines, birthdays, Daily Brief, parking meter, bedtime, Price Watch, bills and warranties, autoruns, and your own helpers). Each has a Test run. They are scheduled on your phone
  • Calendar and Reminders: Tenzy opens the system's own "add event" screen, or adds the tasks you choose. Nothing is read back
  • Android only: Draw over other apps (floating buttons) and Notification access (Notification Log). Tenzy uses no Accessibility service
Each permission is asked for by the helper that needs it. You can change it any time in your phone's Settings.

Autoruns

If you switch one on (My stuff › Set an autorun), Tenzy looks at what's new on your phone once or twice a day — or once a week — while your phone sits idle: the day's photos, videos and PDFs, your contacts for the weekly copy, or the voice memos in the folder you chose. It reads them on your phone only and gets the results ready for you to check. Nothing is added, saved, sent, posted or deleted until you tap, and iPhone asks before anything is deleted. Voice memos are transcribed on the phone only. The weekly contacts copy is a file in Files › Tenzy (and Tenzy's iCloud Drive folder, if you use iCloud Drive). Nothing is uploaded to us.

To suggest an autorun, Tenzy remembers on which days you opened each helper over the last month — on your phone only, and nothing about what was in them.

Network use

  • See & Read and the "Read the page at a link" step download a web page only when you give its link
  • Price Watch downloads the web pages you added, a few times a day when iOS allows, to read the number you asked about. Only the page request is sent, like visiting the site in a browser
  • Daily Brief's weather comes from Apple Weather, and travel times from Apple Maps, using your approximate location only when you have allowed location
  • Android: the speed test downloads a test file from Cloudflare (speed.cloudflare.com) only when you tap start, so Cloudflare sees your IP address like any website you visit. Device scans stay on your own Wi-Fi
  • Purchases, described below

No ads

Tenzy shows no ads at all, free or Pro. There is no advertising SDK, no advertising identifier is used, and there is no App Tracking Transparency prompt. Revenue comes only from the optional Tenzy Pro subscription.

Payments

Tenzy Pro is $2.99 a month or $29.99 a year, each starting with a 7-day free trial, auto-renewing, billed by the App Store or Google Play. If you joined earlier at $19.99 a year, you keep that price. We never see your card. Cancel any time in your Apple ID or Google Play subscription settings.

On-device AI

Text Studio, Memory, Message Shield and the other AI helpers use the model built into your phone where supported. Nothing you type or scan is sent to us or to an AI service.

Think it through can use Apple's Private Cloud Compute when a question needs more than your phone can handle. Your question goes to Apple's servers only to make that answer; Apple doesn't keep it, and neither do we.

Prefer everything to stay on your phone? Turn off "Use Apple's private cloud" in Settings.

Children

Tenzy isn't for children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.
