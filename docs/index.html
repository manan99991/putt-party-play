Tenzy

Set it once. It's handled. A personal assistant for your medicines, bills, birthdays, paperwork, prices and your phone itself. All on your phone, no account.

What it does

  • Build your own helper: describe it in a sentence, or start from a ready-made one
  • Set it once: medicine reminders, birthdays, a morning Daily Brief, parking-meter and price alerts, each with a Test run
  • Contacts, organized: search, groups, favorites, bulk edit, export, merge duplicates
  • Paperwork: scan, sign, fill and redact PDFs; bills and warranties filed with reminders
  • Photos, video and sound: shrink to a size limit, convert, strip location, stickers, video with scene settings, voice changer, meeting notes
  • Everyday helpers: flashlight with SOS, AR tape measure, translate, scam-text check, a Face ID vault

Free and Pro

Seven everyday tools are free: Flashlight, QR & Wi-Fi, See & Read, Measure, Cleaner, Translate and the Face ID Vault, and you can build and keep two tools of your own. The rest are Tenzy Pro: $19.99 a year with a 3-day free trial, unlocking every tool, every builder step and unlimited saved tools. There are no ads. Most of it runs on your phone, with no account and no sign-up.
