Tenzy

Set it once. It's handled. A personal assistant that lives on your phone: tell it what matters, like your medicines, the bill due Friday, a birthday or the price you're waiting for, and it takes care of it from then on. Every reminder has a Test run, so you see it work before you rely on it. No account, nothing uploaded.

Your day, looked after

  • Daily Brief: one note each morning with today's doses, bills due, birthdays and the weather
  • Medicine reminders with a Taken button, a refill countdown and a follow-up if a dose is missed
  • Birthdays from your contacts, a day early; a bedtime nudge; your parking meter on the Lock Screen and where you parked

Money & paperwork

  • Snap a bill or letter: it's filed, with a reminder before it's due; receipts kept, with a nudge before a warranty ends
  • Scan to PDF, sign, fill in forms, black out private details
  • Price Watch tells you when a price on a web page drops
  • Run a small business? Invoices and quotes, a mileage log and a price list

Your phone, looked after

  • Autoruns: switch one on and, once or twice a day, Tenzy gets the heavy work ready for you to check, like today's receipts in your expense sheet or this week's photos as a short film
  • Clean up screenshots and duplicate photos and videos across your whole library
  • Open the files people send you, shrink photos, videos and PDFs to fit an upload limit, strip location before you share
  • Build your own helper: say what you need, or pick a ready-made one, and run it from the share sheet, a widget or Shortcuts

Free and Pro

Thirteen everyday helpers are free, including Scan, Translate, What is this?, Find my car and the Flashlight, and you can build and keep two helpers of your own. Tenzy Pro unlocks everything else, unlimited helpers and all themes: $2.99 a month or $29.99 a year, each starting with a 7-day free trial. If you joined earlier at $19.99 a year, you keep that price. There are no ads, and most of it runs on your phone, with no account and no sign-up.
