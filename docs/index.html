Oddbox Privacy Policy

Short version: everything you do in Oddbox stays on your phone.

Stays on your phone

  • Text you copy while Multi-Clipboard is on (Android), or clips you save via the keyboard or Share Sheet (iOS)
  • Screenshots and screen text you capture
  • Alarms you set
  • Notifications, for 7 days, while Notification Log is on (Android)
  • Your scratchpad or sticky note
  • Scans, extracted text and cleaned photos — until you share them yourself
Stored in the app's private storage. Deleting the app deletes all of it. We can't see it; there is no server.

Never collected

  • Your name, email, or any account — there isn't one
  • Passwords or keystrokes. The Android accessibility service ignores password fields; the iOS Clips keyboard never logs what you type
  • Your location, contacts, files, or photo library beyond the single photo you pick
  • Analytics or crash reports — Oddbox has none

The one exception: ads

  • The free version shows ads through Google AdMob.
  • AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier and basic device information to pick and count ads, under Google's privacy policy.
  • Android: reset or delete the ID in Settings › Google › Ads. iOS: personalised ads are shown only if you allow tracking when asked; change it in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Oddbox Pro removes the ads and the ad code doesn't run.

Payments

  • Pro is $9.99 a year, auto-renewing, billed by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your card.

Permissions

  • Each one is asked for by the tool that needs it, with the reason next to the switch.
  • Android — Accessibility: Multi-Clipboard and full-page screenshots only. Notification access: Notification Log only. Draw over other apps: the floating buttons.
  • iOS — Microphone: Decibel Meter measures level only, never records. Camera: scanner, QR, flashlight. Keyboard Full Access: reads the clipboard only when you tap Grab.

Children

  • Oddbox isn't for children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.
