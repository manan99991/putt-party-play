HOW THE APP WORKS
Tenzy is 10 on-device power tools, each a hub with tabs: Capture, Documents, Media Studio, Clean Up, Voice Notes, Translate, Instruments, Flashlight+, Safety, Share & Connect. Four are always free: Flashlight+, Instruments, Clean Up and Translate. The rest are part of Tenzy Pro.

TENZY PRO (in-app subscription)
- A single auto-renewing subscription: "Tenzy Pro", 1 year, US$19.99, with a 3-day free trial. There is no monthly plan.
- It removes ads and, for end users, unlocks the Pro tools.
- The purchase screen (Settings > Go Pro, or the unlock screen on any Pro tool) clearly shows the price, the 3-day free-trial terms, the auto-renewal disclosure, and functional links to the Terms of Use (EULA) and Privacy Policy, per Guideline 3.1.2.
- The purchase can be completed in the sandbox from that screen. This review build additionally unlocks every Pro tool without a purchase so you can assess the full app.

PERMISSIONS (all processed on-device; nothing is uploaded)
- Contacts: Clean Up › Contacts finds duplicates, merges them without losing a number, exports a .vcf backup; Capture saves a scanned business card. Requested only when used.
- Microphone: Voice Notes (record / live captions / transcription), Instruments › Sound meter, Translate › Conversation. Audio is metered or transcribed on-device; a recording is saved only when the user taps Record.
- Camera: Capture, Documents › Scan, Media Studio, Instruments (magnifier, AR measure), Translate › Camera, Safety › QR, Share & Connect › Scan, Flashlight+ › Mirror.
- Photos: Media Studio, Clean Up and Capture read photos the user selects; Clean Up moves what the user chooses to Recently Deleted.
- Motion / Location: Instruments (level, compass, altitude, GPS dashboard).
- Local network: Share & Connect › Send a file serves the chosen file to another device on the same Wi-Fi.
- Face ID: unlocks the private Vault inside Safety.
- Tracking (ATT): shown only to request personalized ads via AdMob; declining does not limit any feature.

The on-device AI tools use Apple's Foundation Models framework on supported devices and fall back gracefully elsewhere. No data is collected except the AdMob advertising identifier, and Tenzy Pro disables ads entirely.
