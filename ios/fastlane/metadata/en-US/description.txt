Set it once. It's handled.

Tenzy is a personal assistant that lives on your phone. Tell it what matters, like your medicines, the bill due Friday, Mum's birthday or the price you're waiting for, and it takes care of it from then on. Every reminder has a Test run, so you see it work before you rely on it. No account, nothing uploaded.

YOUR DAY, LOOKED AFTER
• Daily Brief: one note each morning with today's doses, bills due, birthdays and the weather
• Medicine Reminders: a Taken button, a refill countdown, and a follow-up if a dose is missed, with one tap to text a caregiver
• Birthdays from your contacts, a day early
• A bedtime nudge, and a parking meter alert that tells you how far away your car is

MONEY & PAPERWORK
• Snap a bill or letter: it's filed, with a reminder before it's due
• Receipts kept, with a nudge before a warranty ends
• Scan to PDF, sign, fill in forms, black out private details
• Price Watch: get told when a price on a web page drops
• Export a year of paperwork in one zip for your accountant

PEOPLE
• Your contacts, organized: search, groups, favorites and emergency contacts, edit many at once, export to Excel, merge duplicates
• Plan a call or a visit: it goes in your calendar with a "time to leave" alert, the number and the address

YOUR PHONE, LOOKED AFTER
• Clean up screenshots and duplicates, keep just your newest ones
• Open the files people send you, even unusual formats
• Shrink photos, videos and PDFs to fit an upload limit, and convert HEIC and MOV
• Strip location from photos before you share them, and check a suspicious text for scams

BUILD YOUR OWN HELPER
Say what you need ("read this letter out loud in English", "shrink a photo, remove the location and email it") and Tenzy puts the steps together for you, or pick a ready-made one. Run it from the share sheet, a widget or Shortcuts, and set "tell me when" alerts on its result.

AND EVERYTHING ELSE YOU REACH FOR
A camera that films with scene settings, a voice changer, stickers and collages, a flashlight with SOS, an AR tape measure that works out the paint and flooring for a room, Translate, white noise, Color Vision, a GPS dashboard and a Face ID vault.

On supported iPhones the thinking and writing tools use the model built into your phone. Available in English, German, Spanish, French, Hindi, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese and Chinese.

Tenzy reminds you; it doesn't give medical advice. Always check with your doctor or pharmacist about your medicines.
