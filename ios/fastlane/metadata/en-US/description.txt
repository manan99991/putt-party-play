Tenzy is ten powerful tools in one app — the jobs your phone usually makes you download a separate app for, with no sign-ups and no "upload to our servers." Everything runs right on your iPhone.

I built it because my home screen had become a graveyard of single-use utilities: one to shrink a photo under an email limit, one to scan a PDF, one to read a sign in another language, one to check whether a text was a scam. Tenzy is all of them in one place, built around ten tools that go deep instead of thirty that go thin.

THE TEN TOOLS
• Capture — point the camera at a receipt, bill, business card, flyer or document and Tenzy does the next step: save the receipt and its total, add the event to your calendar, save the contact, pull a table into a spreadsheet, translate it, or read it aloud.
• Documents — scan pages to a clean, searchable PDF, sign and fill, merge, split and compress — and Word Finder, which searches a word or phrase across all your PDFs, Word, Excel and text files at once.
• Media Studio — get any photo or video ready to send, post or print: shrink to an exact size, remove or swap the background, redact faces and private details, strip location data, make a passport photo, convert HEIC and MOV.
• Clean Up — free real space: duplicate and look-alike photos, the screenshot pile, big videos — plus tidy duplicate contacts. Everything goes to Recently Deleted first, never deleted outright.
• Voice Notes — record or import, get a transcript on your phone, a summary and your to-dos, trim a clip, keep it in a searchable library. Live Captions for any conversation.
• Translate — text, camera and two-way conversation, offline once a language is downloaded.
• Instruments — your phone's toolbox: level, compass, sound and light meters, altimeter, metal detector, ruler, AR tape measure, magnifier and GPS dashboard.
• Flashlight+ — brightness control, strobe, SOS and a Morse flasher, a full-screen lamp, a colour lamp and a selfie mirror.
• Safety — paste a suspicious text, link or number, or scan a QR code, and see whether it's a scam and exactly why. Plus a Face ID vault for private photos and notes.
• Share & Connect — make and scan QR and Wi-Fi codes, and send a file to any nearby device over Wi-Fi — no cable, no cloud.

FREE TOOLS, WITH TENZY PRO FOR THE REST
Flashlight+, Instruments, Clean Up and Translate are free. The rest are part of the optional Tenzy Pro subscription — start with a 3-day free trial, then $19.99/year, and it removes every ad. One person builds and maintains this app; Pro is what keeps it going.

PRIVACY
Your files stay on your device. There's no account and no tracking of what you do inside the app. The only data collected is the standard advertising identifier used by the ad provider to show and count ads — and Pro turns the ads off entirely.
