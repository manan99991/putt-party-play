# What to try in this TestFlight build (26 Nov)

1. My stuff › Set an autorun — switch one on and tap **Test run**. Try: today's receipts, paper into PDFs, the day's collage,
   heavy videos made smaller, clips ready to post, business cards, tidy up, the week's film, the weekly contacts copy,
   voice memos (pick the folder your memos are saved to first). If a Test run ever looks stuck (its timer stops),
   tell me which one — it happened once on the test machine and hasn't happened again since.
2. Home — the camera button: take a picture of a receipt, a card or a page and see what Tenzy offers.
3. Home — the ask box (type or tap the mic): "every evening, help me email today's screenshots to myself" should build
   "Email today's screenshots to myself", with the picker opening on your screenshots.
4. Long Screenshot (should be upright, no status-bar seam) and the photo picker (tap the lower half of a photo — it picks that one).
5. Cleaner › Duplicates — it checks your whole library now and says how much it checked. Only true copies are shown
   (the two calculator screenshots are no longer called copies). Ticking every copy deletes every copy.
6. Got something? → a photo → Send it → Privacy Scrubber: your photo should already be in it.
7. Home › See all: helpers by section, each section's ready-made helpers, and "Make your own".
8. Settings: every line fits on one line ("Looks after your phone").
9. Settings › Help › Watch how: three short videos filmed in Tenzy — say what you need, set an autorun, got something?
