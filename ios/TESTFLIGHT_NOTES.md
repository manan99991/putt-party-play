# What to try in this TestFlight build (27 Nov)

New: **Video Studio** (Home › Video; it was "Video Effects") — one place for a video:

- **Faces** tab: 36 effects on every face, steady from frame to frame — Cone head, Egg head, Alien, Balloon face,
  big/tiny head, big mouth, big eyes, squished, chipmunk cheeks; shades, heart glasses, crown, moustache, puppy, bunny,
  clown nose, halo, devil horns, party hat, flower crown; Zombie, Robot, Cartoon, Glow; Beautiful, Seriously sunny,
  Tacky, Wholesome; Long legs, Big head tiny body. Each one shows on your own video before you pick it.
- **Background**: blur, colour pop, backdrops — or **Your photo** behind you.
- **Music**: Tenzy's own music (Upbeat, Party, Chill, Epic), **Your song** from Files, or No sound, with a slider
  between the music and the video's own sound. You hear it in the preview.
- When it's made: save it, or tap where it's going (TikTok, Reels, Shorts…) and it's made the right shape and opened.

Please try the face effects on a real face and tell me which ones look off — the test machine's face finder is
unreliable, so the real phone is the first true test of how well they sit.

Also in this build — the video and voice round:

1. **Movie Trailer** (Home › Video): pick or film a few clips, name the film, tap **Make my trailer** — title cards,
   a deep narrator ("In a world…"), slow motion, a montage that builds, the title on a huge hit, "Coming soon".
   Change the narrator's lines if you like.
2. **Beat Reel** (Home › Video): a few clips and photos, pick Upbeat, Party, Chill or Epic — every cut lands on a beat.
3. **Funny Moments** (Home › Video): record scratch "Yep. That's me.", dramatic zoom, crickets, air horns, boing,
   ba-dum-tss. Tenzy finds the moment (the loudest laugh); slide to move it.
4. **Make it stunning** on a video › the new **Background** tab (blur, colour pop, backdrops) and **Funny** tab
   (big head, tiny head, big eyes, stretchy, wobbly).
5. **Change the voice**: four new voices — auto-tune singer, stadium announcer, old radio, whisper.
6. **Ask box**: "remind me to call Mum at 6", "remind me tomorrow morning about the dentist", "remind me every Monday
   to put the bins out" — set in one tap, with Test and Undo.
7. **Camera button in every photo and video tool**: Take a photo / Record right there (Photo Studio, Translate,
   Price Check, Fit It, Privacy Scrubber, Ready to Send, Celebrate, Video, Post it, the photo picker…).

All the music and sound effects are made by Tenzy on the phone; the narrator is the phone's own voice.

On Android (the latest test APK): Beat Reel, Movie Trailer, Funny Moments and Video Effects (now with its Background and
Funny tabs) are on the Photos tab; Change the voice has all 19 voices; Build › Describe it sets "remind me…" reminders;
seven tools have a camera button.
