Tenzy is a box of small, handy tools in one app — the stuff your phone makes you dig for, or download a separate app for every time.

I built it because my home screen had filled up with single-use utilities: one app to free up photo space, another to shrink a video small enough to send, another to scan a document to PDF. Tenzy puts them in one place, and most of them run right on your phone — no account, nothing uploaded.

What's inside right now:

EVERYDAY
• Space Cleaner — find duplicate and look-alike photos and clear the screenshot pile (everything goes to a recoverable trash, never deleted outright)
• Storage Map — see exactly what's eating your space
• Contacts Cleanup — merge duplicates and back up your contacts
• My Phone — battery, storage, memory and network at a glance
• Text Tools — tidy up pasted text, fix the case, flatten broken line breaks
• Flashlight — patterns, SOS, strobe
• QR & Barcode — scan and make codes
• Lens — grab text from a photo, or have it read aloud
• Translate — offline and private
• Magnifier — zoom in on small print
• Measure — level, compass and sound meter

DOCUMENTS & MEDIA
• Doc Scanner — scan pages to a clean PDF
• PDF Toolkit — merge, split and compress PDFs
• Shrink — make photos and videos small enough to send
• Background Remover — cut out the subject
• Audio to Text — transcribe a voice recording
• Sign & Fill — sign a document and export a PDF
• Photo Metadata — strip location and EXIF before sharing
• Sorted — snap a bill or letter and drop it into your calendar

SMART (on-device)
• Memory — search your screenshots by what's in them (that Wi-Fi password, a confirmation code)
• Summarize, Rewrite, Proofread, Extract to data, Describe an image, Identify, Draft a reply, Handwriting to text, and Make a sticker

Free to use, with ads. Tenzy Pro removes every ad and unlocks unlimited use — $19.99/year. One person builds and maintains this, and Pro is what keeps it going.

Privacy: your files stay on your device. There's no account and no tracking of what you do in the app. The only data collected is the standard advertising ID the ad provider uses to show and count ads, and Pro turns the ads off entirely.
