Tenzy is a toolkit of powerful utilities in one app — the jobs your phone usually makes you download a separate app for. No sign-ups, no "upload to our servers." Everything runs on your phone. No account, nothing uploaded.

WHAT'S INSIDE

• File Viewer — open and read almost any file: PDFs, images, archives (and look inside a zip), APKs, SQLite databases, code, JSON and CSV, even RAW camera photos and EPUB books. For anything unusual it still shows the real details, text and checksums instead of "no app can open this."

• Docs & Capture — point your camera at a receipt, card, flyer or document and pull out the text, table or contact; scan pages to a clean PDF; sign and fill; merge, split and compress; and find a word across all your PDFs, Word, Excel and text files at once.

• Converter — turn any file into a format that works: convert between image types, HEIC to JPG, images into a PDF and back, and transcode audio and video.

• Media Studio — get a photo or video ready to send, post or print: shrink to size, remove or swap the background, redact, make an ID photo, convert HEIC, and strip location data.

• Scrubber — hide personal details automatically. It finds faces and any visible text — licence plates, names, addresses, numbers — and blurs them, strips the photo's location and metadata, and lets you brush over anything else. Photos, plus a best-effort pass for short videos.

• Clean Up — free real space: duplicate and look-alike photos, the screenshot pile, big videos and files, plus tidy duplicate contacts. Everything goes to a recoverable trash.

• Codes — scan any QR or barcode and act on it, make your own QR for a link, Wi-Fi network, contact or text, and read NFC tags.

• Instruments — your phone's toolbox: level, compass, sound and light meters, altimeter, metal detector, ruler, magnifier, and a full flashlight (strobe, SOS and Morse, screen light and mirror).

• Safety — paste a suspicious text, link or number, or scan a QR code, and see whether it's a scam and exactly why.

• Share & Connect — make and scan QR and Wi-Fi codes, and send a file to a nearby device over Wi-Fi — no cable, no cloud.
