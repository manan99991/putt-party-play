Tenzy is ten powerful tools in one app — the jobs your phone usually makes you download a separate app for, with no sign-ups and no "upload to our servers." Everything runs right on your phone. No account, nothing uploaded.

THE TEN TOOLS

• Capture — point your camera at a receipt, bill, business card, flyer or document, and Tenzy does the next step for you: add the event to your calendar, save the contact, log the receipt and its warranty date, pull a table into a spreadsheet, translate it, or read it aloud.

• Documents — scan pages to a clean PDF, sign and fill, merge, split and compress, and find a word or phrase across all your PDFs, Word, Excel and text files at once.

• Media Studio — get any photo or video ready to send, post or print: shrink it to size, remove or swap the background, redact faces and private details, strip location data, make a passport/ID photo, and convert HEIC.

• Clean Up — free real space: duplicate and look-alike photos, the screenshot pile, big videos and files — plus tidy up duplicate contacts. Everything goes to a recoverable trash, never deleted outright.

• Voice Notes — record or import audio, get a transcript on your phone, tidy it up, trim a clip, and keep it in a searchable library.

• Translate — text, camera and two-way conversation, offline once a language is downloaded.

• Instruments — your phone's toolbox: level, compass, sound meter, light meter, altimeter, metal detector, ruler and magnifier.

• Flashlight+ — brightness control, SOS and a Morse message flasher, a full-screen lamp, and a selfie mirror.

• Safety — paste a suspicious text, link or number, or scan a QR code, and see whether it's a scam and exactly why.

• Share & Connect — make and scan QR and Wi-Fi codes, and send a file to any nearby device over Wi-Fi — no cable, no cloud.

Free to use, with ads. Tenzy Pro removes every ad and unlocks unlimited use — $19.99/year. One person builds and maintains this, and Pro is what keeps it going.

Privacy: your files stay on your device. There's no account and no tracking of what you do in the app. The only data collected is the standard advertising ID the ad provider uses to show and count ads, and Pro turns the ads off entirely.
