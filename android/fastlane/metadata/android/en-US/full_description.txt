Tenzy is 33 small tools in one app: the jobs your phone usually makes you download a separate app for. No account, no sign-up, and most of it runs right on your phone.

PHOTO, VIDEO & FILES
• Media Studio: shrink a photo or video to a size limit, remove or swap the background, redact, make an ID photo, convert HEIC, strip location data. Keeps full resolution, including 4K video
• Converter: convert between image types, HEIC to JPG, images to PDF and back, transcode audio and video
• GIF Maker: turn a video into a looping GIF, up to 15 seconds
• Scrubber: blur faces and visible text, strip a photo's location and metadata
• File Viewer: open almost any file, including PDFs, archives, databases, code and e-books
• Pro Camera: manual ISO and shutter
• Long Screenshot: stitch screenshots into one tall image

DOCUMENTS & EVERYDAY
• Docs & Capture: scan pages to a clean PDF, pull out text, sign and fill, merge, split and compress
• Data Grabber, Receipts and Sorted: turn a receipt, bill or table photo into rows, warranties and calendar dates
• Invoice: make a quick invoice to send
• Text Tools: clean and change text
• Translate: text, camera and conversation
• Voice: record, transcribe and trim
• Memory: ask your screenshots anything

PHONE & TOOLBOX
• Clean Up: find duplicate photos and big files, tidy duplicate contacts; deletions go to a recoverable trash
• Storage and My Phone: see what's filling your phone and your device details
• Codes and Share & Connect: scan and make QR codes, Wi-Fi codes and file transfer
• Network: speed test, Wi-Fi and devices
• Safety: spot scam messages and bad links
• Instruments: level, compass, sound and light meters, altimeter, metal detector
• Flashlight: torch, patterns, Morse, screen light
• Hearing Boost, GPS Dashboard, Where I Parked, Rest (sleep sounds), Color Vision, Identify, See & Read, Price Check and Quick Buttons

FREE TO DOWNLOAD, WITH OPTIONAL TENZY PRO
Every tool is free to try with daily limits on the heavier jobs. Optional Tenzy Pro is $19.99 a year, billed by Google Play: it removes the ads and the daily limits and unlocks the extra themes. Cancel any time in Google Play.

PRIVACY
Your files stay on your phone. There is no account and no analytics. The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, which may use your advertising ID; Pro turns the ads off. Each permission (camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos) is asked for only by the tool that needs it. Full policy: https://manan99991.github.io/tenzy/privacy.html
