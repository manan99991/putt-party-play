Mini Golf Guardians Privacy Policy

Short version: your game progress stays on your phone. There is no account and no sign-up. Ads are shown by Unity Ads, and we ask for non-personalised ads only.

Stays on your phone

  • Your progress: holes played, scores, stars, gems, coins, unlocked worlds and the items you own
  • Your settings: sound effects, music, vibration and hole cards
  • Daily missions, your login streak, and a small on-device log of ads shown (used only to keep ads fair, for example the daily limit on "watch an ad for coins")
Stored in the app's private storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes all of it. We can't see it.

Never collected by us

  • Your name, email or any account. There isn't one
  • Your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. The game never asks for them
  • Analytics or tracking of how you play

Ads (Unity Ads)

The free game shows a short ad after every 3 holes, and you can choose to watch an ad for a reward (extra coins, a mulligan, double coins or a free bonus round). Ads are provided by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). We ask Unity for non-personalised ads only and mark the game as not directed to children under 13. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Unity may receive information such as your device type, operating system, IP address and how you interact with an ad. Unity's use of that information is covered by Unity's privacy policy. The "Remove Ads" purchase turns off the ads between holes. Ads you choose to watch for a reward stay available.

Purchases

Purchases (worlds, coins, items, Remove Ads and Guardians Plus) are handled by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. The store tells the game only that a purchase went through, so it can unlock what you bought. Coins and items are part of the game, have no cash value and can't be exchanged for money.

Network use

  • The game downloads its pictures, music and an ad settings file from our website (hosted on GitHub Pages). Like any website visit, the host sees your IP address
  • The world menu reads hole layouts from our game database (Google Firebase). Nothing about you is sent to it
  • Ads, as described above

Children

Mini Golf Guardians is rated for ages 13 and up and is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

  • Turn sound effects, music and vibration on or off in Settings at any time
  • See what ads do in Settings › Privacy & ads
  • Delete the app to delete everything it stored
